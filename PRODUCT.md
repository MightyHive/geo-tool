# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal data specialists assessing generative engine optimisation (GEO) for clients. They need to investigate audit evidence efficiently and turn the findings into a clear client-facing report.

## Product Purpose

Help specialists assess a client's visibility and readiness across search and generative AI surfaces, understand the supporting evidence, and communicate actionable findings to the client.

## Brand Personality

Informative, interactive, user-friendly.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic, card-heavy SaaS styling, decorative interactions, and visual treatments that make dense audit evidence harder to scan or trust.

## Design Principles

- Make evidence easy to inspect and verify.
- Keep specialist workflows efficient and predictable.
- Translate technical findings into client-ready language.
- Use interaction to reveal useful detail, not decoration.
- Preserve trust through clear provenance and honest data availability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, semantic structure, and usable experiences without relying on colour or motion alone.
